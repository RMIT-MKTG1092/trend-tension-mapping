Trend, tension and opportunity worksheet

What this worksheet helps you do

You will take a trend (a change in the world) and turn it into a clear opportunity for innovation. The key step is finding the tension.

A tension is a clash. People want something, but something gets in the way. The “gets in the way” part is often time, money, trust, effort, confusion, or access.

How to use this

  • Start with one strong trend signal from your PESTLE work.
  • Write the tension in one clear sentence (people want… but…).
  • Write an innovation prompt (a direction, not a final product).
  • Add a short note about what you would test first.
  • Add a source to prove the trend is real.

Example (structure only)

Trend signal: More people want solutions that are easy to use at home.
Tension: People want help quickly, but they do not want extra cost or hassle.
Innovation prompt: How might we make it easy to get support at home without wasting time or money?
